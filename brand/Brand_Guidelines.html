Brand Guidelines · v1 · June 2026

Valore Property Holdings

A boutique identity built on worth, trust and quiet confidence.

The idea

“Valore” is Italian for worth and value. The brand sits deliberately in the premium register of names like Knight Frank and Coutts — heritage, restraint, competence — while staying warm and human. In a sector where most operators look amateur or exploitative, looking and acting trustworthy is the competitive advantage. Promise: homes that matter, run properly, for partners who can rely on us.

Logo

Primary lockup is the wordmark with crest. The crest monogram (gold serif “V”, diamond apex) is used standalone for avatars, favicons, document seals and the app. Always keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the “V”. Never stretch, recolour outside the palette, add shadows, or place the navy mark on a busy photo without a scrim.

Colour

Valore Navy#0F2440 · primary
Valore Gold#B8862E · accent
Light Gold#D9A948 · highlight
Ivory#F7F4ED · ground
Ink#1c2430 · text
Slate#6b7785 · muted

Navy carries authority and stability; gold signals worth, used sparingly as rules, accents and the mark — never large fills. Ivory (not stark white) gives the warm, premium ground. Charcoal ink for body text.

Typography

Display / headings — refined serif
Homes that matter
Stack: Georgia, “Times New Roman”, serif. (Optional web font: Playfair Display / Cormorant for digital.)
Body / UI — clean sans
Calm, competent, plain-speaking copy that respects the reader.
Stack: system sans (-apple-system, Segoe UI, Arial).
