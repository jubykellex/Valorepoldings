Questions

The things landlords ask first

What if the tenant doesn't pay? +
You are paid by the provider, not the individual tenant. You receive the agreed rent on the agreed date regardless of occupancy or whether an end-tenant falls into arrears.
How is my property protected? +
We hold the appropriate agreements and licences, inspect regularly, keep all safety certificates current, and manage maintenance. The property is returned to you in good order at the end of the term.
What kind of properties do you take? +
Houses and flats in areas with strong social-housing demand — primarily Northern and Midlands markets, assessed case by case. Tell us the address and we'll be straight with you about fit.
How long are the agreements? +
Typically three to five years, sometimes longer. We agree clear written terms up front, with heads of terms captured in a letter of intent before anything is committed.
