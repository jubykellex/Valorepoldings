Questions

What investors ask first

What returns can I expect? +
Every deal is underwritten individually and we never promise a fixed return. We share the full numbers, our assumptions and the downside before you commit. The indicative model above shows the kind of structures we work with.
How is my capital protected? +
Through conservative underwriting, written agreements defining roles and security, and disciplined deal selection. We buy at a margin and stress-test for rate rises and lower valuations.
What does reporting look like? +
A dedicated investor portal with your deals, documents and returns, plus quarterly written updates. If something changes, you hear it from us first.
Are you compliant and regulated? +
Yes — see Governance & compliance. We register for HMRC AML supervision, join a property redress scheme, register with the ICO, and license every qualifying HMO. This is not a regulated investment offer; it is information about how we work.
